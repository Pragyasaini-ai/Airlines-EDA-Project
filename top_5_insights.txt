AIRLINES EDA — TOP 5 KEY INSIGHTS

1. Overall Passenger Satisfaction
Out of 103,904 passenger records, 43.33% were satisfied, while 56.67% were neutral or dissatisfied.

2. Business Class Satisfaction
Business Class had the highest satisfaction rate at 69.43%.

3. Economy Class Satisfaction
Eco Class had the lowest satisfaction rate at 18.61%, indicating an area for further investigation.

4. Average Flight Delays
Average departure delay was 14.82 minutes, while average arrival delay was 15.13 minutes.

5. Delays Exceeding 15 Minutes
There were 23,052 departure-delay records and 23,635 arrival-delay records exceeding 15 minutes.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
- Investigate factors linked to low satisfaction in Eco Class.
- Review passenger service ratings and identify areas for improvement.
- Monitor departure and arrival delays.
- Investigate long delays using additional operational data.

DATA LIMITATIONS
The dataset does not contain loyalty-program details or specific delay reasons. Therefore, these topics could not be directly analyzed in this project.
